## Facts (as narrated)
- GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026; on PS5 it is a rolling midnight launch by country, starting in New Zealand (Sony, via Forbes).
- Rockstar: the preload "will require up to 212 GB depending on your device"; PS5 preload screens showed about 209.75 GB.
- 212 GB = 1,696 gigabits. At 100 Mbps that is about 17,000 s, roughly 4.7 h; at 20 Mbps it is roughly 23.6 h (ideal speeds).
- 212 GB does not fit on a 128 GB phone.
- Preload opened November 10, 2026, nine days before launch.
- GTA 5 (2013) made $815.7 million in its first 24 hours and $1 billion in three days, a Guinness World Record (fastest entertainment property to reach $1 billion).
- Analysts expect GTA 6's launch to beat that (for example, forecasts of several billion dollars in launch sales); this is a forecast, not a fact.
