## Facts (approximate, as narrated)
- GTA VI launches on November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S; no PC date announced.
- Reported download size about 210 GB on PS5 and 225 GB on Xbox. Many phones have 128 GB.
- PS5 GPU ≈ 10.3 TFLOPS; the fastest phone GPUs ≈ 2–3 TFLOPS (Apple A17 Pro ≈ 2.1).
- Phones slow themselves down when hot (thermal throttling). On-screen temperature and FPS values are illustrative.
- A PS5 draws about 200 W while gaming; a phone battery holds about 19–20 Wh (5,000 mAh × 3.85 V): 19.5 Wh ÷ 200 W ≈ 6 minutes.
- Cloud gaming runs the game on a server and streams only the video to the device.
- GTA V launched in 2013 on PS3/Xbox 360; the PS3 GPU is about 0.2 TFLOPS, so a modern phone GPU is several times stronger.
