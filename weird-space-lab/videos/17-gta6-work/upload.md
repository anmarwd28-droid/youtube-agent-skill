## Facts and maths (approximate)
Price: GTA 6 Standard Edition US$79.99 (Take-Two/Rockstar). Rates around 16 Nov 2026: €1 = $1.10, £1 = $1.32, $1 = R$5.7, $1 = EGP 53.7, $1 ≈ ₦1,350 (indirect estimate).
- Germany: minimum wage €13.90/h (2026). $79.99 ≈ €72.7, about 5.2 h.
- UK: National Living Wage £12.71/h (April 2026). $79.99 ≈ £60.6, about 4.8 h.
- USA: federal minimum $7.25/h. About 11 h (many states pay more).
- Brazil: R$1,621/month (2026), 220 h/month. $79.99 ≈ R$456, 28% of a month, about 62 h, roughly 8 days of 8 h.
- Egypt: private-sector minimum EGP 7,000/month. $79.99 ≈ EGP 4,295, 61% of a month, about 128 h at 208 h/month, roughly 16 days of 8 h, more than half a month.
- Nigeria: national minimum ₦70,000/month. $79.99 ≈ ₦108,000, about 1.5 months, about 267 h at 173 h/month. The naira rate is uncertain; a weaker naira makes it longer.
- Regional store prices differ from the US price ("store prices change a little from country to country").
