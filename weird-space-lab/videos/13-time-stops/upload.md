## Facts (a thought experiment, as narrated)
- We see because light (photons) travels from objects into our eyes. If light stopped moving, no new light would reach your retina, so you would see nothing.
- Sound is a pressure wave carried by moving air molecules. With frozen air, no sound could travel.
- Breathing and moving need air molecules to move out of the way. Frozen in place, air would behave like an immovable solid around you.
- If everything except you stopped, the world would carry on afterwards exactly as before, so no one else would notice.
- This is a thought experiment: real physics has no way to stop time for everything but one person.
