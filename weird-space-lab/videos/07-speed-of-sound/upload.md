## Facts (approximate, as narrated)
- The speed of sound in air at 20 °C is about 343 m/s, which is about 1,235 km/h.
- Usain Bolt's top measured speed is about 12.4 m/s (44.7 km/h). 343 ÷ 12.4 ≈ 28, so "almost thirty times faster".
- Drag F = ½·ρ·v²·Cd·A. With ρ = 1.2 kg/m³, v = 343 m/s and Cd·A ≈ 0.7 m² for a person, F ≈ 49,000 N. That is the weight of about 5 tonnes.
- An object moving faster than sound makes a shock wave. A bullet passing nearby makes a sharp "crack" for this reason.
- A typical human reaction time is about 0.25 s. 343 m/s × 0.25 s ≈ 86 m.
- Air stopped against the front of a fast-moving body heats up by about v²/(2·cp) = 343² / (2 × 1005) ≈ 58 °C (stagnation heating).
