## Facts (numbers and sources)
- Adults pass about 0.5–1.5 L of gas a day, about 10–20 times (NHS; Tomlin et al., Gut 1991 ≈ 14 times/day, ≈ 0.7 L).
- Most is made by trillions of colon bacteria fermenting food (H₂, CO₂, CH₄).
- Trapped gas causes distension, pain and cramps; part of intestinal gas diffuses into the blood and is exhaled by the lungs.
- Hydrogen breath tests diagnose lactose intolerance and bacterial overgrowth (SIBO).
- Some volatile gut compounds reach the breath and add to bad breath.
- After abdominal surgery, the first flatus is a sign the bowel has recovered from postoperative ileus.
- Herring make "Fast Repetitive Tick" sounds by releasing air bubbles from the anal duct, likely to communicate in the dark (Wilson, Batty & Dill, Biology Letters 2004).
