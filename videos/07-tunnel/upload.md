## Facts (approximate, as narrated)
- Earth's diameter is about 12,742 km; the deepest hole ever drilled (Kola Superdeep Borehole) is about 12.3 km.
- Mantle temperatures reach thousands of °C and the mantle flows very slowly; the inner core is about 5,200–6,000 °C, close to the Sun's surface (~5,500 °C).
- In an airless tunnel through a uniform-density Earth you would pass the centre at about 7.9 km/s and reach the far side in about 42 minutes, then oscillate forever.
