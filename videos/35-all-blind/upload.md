# ماذا لو أصبح كل البشر عُمياناً فجأة؟ — Short #35

63.2 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps. Global framing.

What it shows, in order:
1. The real 3D Earth; eyelids close over the whole view at once; chip "٨ مليارات إنسان… بلا بصر".
2. The globe with hundreds of tiny planes moving; they turn red; a round inset of a pilot in the cockpit, eyes shut, rubbing his eyes.
3. An airliner above the clouds with "الطيّار الآليّ: يعمل"; far below an airport and runway; a dashed glide path ends in a "؟"; the control tower is circled in red and an inset shows the controller, eyes shut, by a glowing radar.
4. A new street (bakery, pharmacy, flowers, café, opticians): people on the pavement feel their way with arms out; a car stops crooked; a blue car runs into a red one: dust, glass, steam, hazard lights.
5. The night side of the Earth: the city lights flicker and go out in steps.
6. A head in profile with the brain: the back third (the visual cortex) glows gold, signals run from the eye; the eye closes and the region goes grey ("بلا عمل"); a pie shows "نحو ثلث قشرة الدماغ".
7. The same region turns teal: a fingertip reads raised dots and sound waves reach the ear, both feeding the old visual area; chip "بعد ٥ أيّامٍ فقط".
8. A blind man with dark glasses and a white cane walks confidently, leading two people (eyes shut) with their hands on his shoulder; chips "٤٣ مليونَ كفيف" and "معلّمو العالم".
9. A hand reads a Braille page (the touched dots light up); then at night the same man clicks his tongue ("طَق"), gold rings go out, echoes come back, and the dark wall ahead is revealed ("الجدار").
10. Earth with the question mark and the subscribe button.

## Facts (as narrated)

- **About 8 billion people.** The world population passed 8 billion in Nov 2022 and is about 8.2 billion in 2025 (UN World Population Prospects 2024).
- **About a million people in the air at any moment.** Airlines carried about 5 billion passengers in 2024–25 (IATA). With an average flight of about 2 hours: 5×10⁹ × 2 h ÷ 8,760 h ≈ 1.1 million people airborne on average. An estimate, so "نحو".
- **Autopilot keeps the plane flying; landing needs eyes.** Autopilots hold height and course for hours. Automatic landing (autoland, CAT III) exists, but the crew must select and set up the approach and air traffic control must clear and separate the traffic, which needs people who can see (FAA AC 120-118; ICAO Annex 6).
- **Millions of cars on the road.** There are about 1.5 billion vehicles in use in the world (OICA); at any moment tens of millions are being driven.
- **Within days, power fails.** Power grids are balanced by operators minute by minute; plants and grids without staff trip and fail within hours to days. Shown as a scenario, not a measurement.
- **About a third of the cortex works on vision.** In humans roughly 20–30% of the cerebral cortex is mainly visual, more than for any other sense (Van Essen, "Organization of visual areas in macaque and human cerebral cortex", 2004; the ~30% figure is widely cited from neuroscience reviews). Narrated as "نحو ثلث".
- **After only 5 days, the visual cortex takes on touch and sound.** Sighted volunteers kept blindfolded for 5 days began to use their visual cortex for Braille-like touch and for sound; it reversed within a day of removing the blindfold (Pascual-Leone & Hamilton, Progress in Brain Research 134, 2001; Merabet et al., PLoS ONE 3(8): e3046, 2008).
- **43 million blind people today.** In 2020 about 43.3 million people worldwide were blind (Bourne et al., Lancet Global Health 9(2): e130–e143, 2021, Vision Loss Expert Group / GBD).
- **Echolocation by tongue clicks.** Some blind people find walls and objects by clicking their tongue and listening to the echoes; in expert echolocators the echoes activate the visual cortex (Thaler, Arnott & Goodale, PLoS ONE 6(5): e20162, 2011).
- **Global framing:** no country is named; the street stands for any city.
