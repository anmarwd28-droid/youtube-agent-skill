## Facts (approximate, as narrated)
- If all land ice melted, sea level would rise about 70 m (USGS ~70 m; often quoted 65–70 m).
- Baghdad lies about 34 m above sea level; Basra, Kuwait City and Doha are only a few metres up.
- About two thirds (~69%) of Earth's fresh water is locked in ice caps and glaciers.
- Melting all of it would take thousands of years (estimates of 5,000+ years).
