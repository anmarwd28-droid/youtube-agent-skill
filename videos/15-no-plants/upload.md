## Facts (approximate, as narrated)
- The atmosphere is ~21% oxygen (~1.2 × 10¹⁸ kg). Breathing and burning fuel consume a tiny fraction per year, so the existing oxygen would last thousands of years (tens of thousands at today's rates) even with no new production.
- Every food chain starts with photosynthesis: grains, rice, dates and vegetables directly, meat and milk via animals that eat plants.
- Food stocks would run out within weeks to months; herbivores starve first, then predators.
- Roots hold soil; without vegetation, wind and rain strip topsoil — dust storms and mud and debris flows.
- Forests return large amounts of water vapour to the air (the Amazon recycles roughly a quarter to a half of its rain), so continental interiors would get much drier.
- Plants and soils absorb a large share of human CO₂ emissions each year; without them CO₂ would rise faster and warm the planet more.
- From space, the green continents would look brown and grey (bare rock and soil).
