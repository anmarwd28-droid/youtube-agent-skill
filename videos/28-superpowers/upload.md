## Facts (as narrated)

- **Lifting a car:** a typical car is about 1,500 kg. Holding it up, its whole weight (about 14,700 N) still passes through your body into the ground, and the ground pushes back with the same force. Superpowers do not switch off physics.
- **Pushing a wall (Newton's third law):** the wall pushes back on you with exactly the force you push it with. Your shoes can only hold about μ·m·g ≈ 0.8 × 70 × 9.8 ≈ 550 N of friction, so a push of thousands of newtons throws you backwards.
- **A ten-storey jump:** ten floors ≈ 30 m.
  - Take-off and landing speed without air: v = √(2gh) = √(2 × 9.81 × 30) ≈ 24.3 m/s ≈ 87 km/h. Time in the air: 2v/g ≈ 4.9 s (shown in real time).
  - With air drag (terminal speed 55–75 m/s) the landing speed is about 83–85 km/h. Narrated as "more than eighty kilometres per hour"; the gauge shows 84.
- **Eye beams that cut steel:** industrial lasers that cut steel sheet are about 1 kW and up (commonly 1–6 kW). A resting human body produces about 100 W (2,000 kcal/day = 8.37 MJ / 86,400 s ≈ 97 W). 1,000 / 100 = 10.
- **Invisibility means blindness:** to see, the retina must absorb light and the lens must bend it. If light passes straight through your body, it also passes through your retina without being absorbed, so no image forms.
- **Eight billion jumpers:** world population is a little over 8 billion.
  - Mass ≈ 8.2 × 10⁹ × 60 kg ≈ 4.9 × 10¹¹ kg; Earth is 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg.
  - If all jumped at 24.3 m/s from one place, Earth recoils at m·v/M ≈ 2 × 10⁻¹² m/s for 4.9 s: about 10⁻¹¹ m, a tenth of the width of a hydrogen atom (≈ 10⁻¹⁰ m). It returns when they land. Spread around the globe, the pushes cancel even more.
- **Your brain:** about 86 billion neurons (Azevedo et al., 2009), running on about 20 W, around 20% of the body's resting power: less than a small light bulb.
