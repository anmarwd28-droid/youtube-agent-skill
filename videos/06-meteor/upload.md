## Facts (approximate, as narrated)
- The Chicxulub impactor was about 10 km wide, hitting at roughly 20 km/s; crater about 180 km wide; 66 million years ago.
- Its energy (~10^8 megatons) exceeded the world's combined nuclear arsenal thousands of times over.
- Impact tsunamis of hundreds of metres, re-entering ejecta igniting fires, and soot/dust blocking sunlight for years (impact winter).
- NASA's DART spacecraft hit Dimorphos in 2022 and shortened its orbit around Didymos by about 32 minutes.
