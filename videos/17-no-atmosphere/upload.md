## Facts (approximate, as narrated)
- The sky is blue because air scatters sunlight (Rayleigh scattering); with no air the daytime sky is black and stars are visible beside the Sun, as on the Moon.
- Sound needs a medium; with no air there is no sound.
- Wings and birds need air for lift.
- With no external pressure a person loses consciousness in roughly 10–15 seconds.
- Water boils when the pressure falls below its vapour pressure, even at room temperature; evaporation then cools the surface until it freezes.
- The ozone layer absorbs most solar ultraviolet; without it the full UV would reach the ground.
- Most meteoroids burn up from air friction; with no atmosphere they would reach the ground.
- Air and clouds keep heat in and spread it; without them days get scorching and nights freezing, like the Moon's surface.
- Most of the atmosphere lies within ~100 km of the surface, about 1.5% of Earth's radius — thinner, relative to Earth, than an apple's skin.
