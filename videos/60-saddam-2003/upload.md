## Facts (as narrated)
- **9 April 2003:** the statue of Saddam Hussein in Firdos Square, Baghdad (put up in 2002), was pulled down as US troops entered the city; the war began on 20 March 2003.
- **About 200,000 civilians:** Iraq Body Count documents about 187,000–211,000 civilian deaths from violence from 2003 to 2023 (the war, the insurgency and sectarian fighting, and the war against ISIS). It is a floor; survey estimates are higher.
- **More than 4 million displaced:** by 2007–2008 UNHCR counted over 2 million Iraqis displaced inside Iraq and about 2 million who had fled abroad.
- **ISIS and Mosul:** the group grew out of al-Qaeda in Iraq (formed in the chaos after 2003) and took Mosul in June 2014; the leaning al-Hadba minaret of the al-Nuri mosque (12th century) was blown up in June 2017. "Maybe" is stated: it is a counterfactual.
- **Sanctions since 1990:** UN Security Council Resolution 661 (6 August 1990) imposed sanctions after the invasion of Kuwait; they were lifted by Resolution 1483 in May 2003.
- **The dinar:** in the 1980s the official rate was about 1 dinar = 3.2 US dollars; by late 1995 the market (black-market) rate reached about 3,000 dinars to the dollar.
- **Oil:** Iraq produced about 2 million barrels a day in 2002 (under sanctions and oil-for-food); in 2024 it averaged about 4.4 million b/d (EIA), about twice as much.
- **Satellite dishes and the internet:** owning a satellite dish was illegal in government-held Iraq (fines and prison); internet access, from about 2000, went only through state centres and was monitored.
- **Since 1979:** Saddam Hussein became president on 16 July 1979.
- **Halabja:** on 16 March 1988 the Iraqi regime attacked the Kurdish town of Halabja with chemical weapons; about 5,000 people were killed (estimates 3,200–5,000), the deadliest chemical attack on civilians.
- **The Arab Spring:** from December 2010 / 2011, protests spread from Tunisia to Egypt, Libya, Yemen and Syria. Whether it would have reached Baghdad is a question, said as one.
- **89 years:** Saddam Hussein was born on 28 April 1937, so he would be 89 in 2026. (He was captured in December 2003 and executed in December 2006.)
- **No weapons found:** the Iraq Survey Group (Duelfer Report, September 2004) found no stockpiles of weapons of mass destruction and concluded Iraq had destroyed them in 1991.
