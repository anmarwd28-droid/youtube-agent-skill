## Facts used (approximate, as said in the narration)
- Without the Moon every night would be as dark as a moonless night today, so many more stars are visible.
- Many animals time hunting or hiding by moonlight; many corals (e.g. the Great Barrier Reef) time mass spawning by the lunar cycle.
- The Moon causes most of the tides; the Sun's tidal effect is a bit under half the Moon's, so tides would shrink to roughly a third of today's typical range.
- The Moon stabilises Earth's axial tilt (about 23.5°); without it the tilt could vary much more over millions of years, causing extreme seasons.
