## Facts (numbers and sources)
- About 117 billion people have ever been born (Population Reference Bureau, "How Many People Have Ever Lived on Earth?", 2022 update; an estimate, hence «نحو»).
- About 8.2 billion people are alive now (UN World Population Prospects 2024). 117 − 8.2 ≈ 109 billion would return; 109 / 8.2 ≈ 13.3, so about 13 for every person alive.
- Shoulder to shoulder at about 0.25 m² per person: 117 × 10⁹ × 0.25 m² ≈ 2.9 × 10¹⁰ m² ≈ 29,000 km². Belgium is about 30,500 km². The gold square on the globe is 1.53° of latitude × 2.4° of longitude at 50.5° N ≈ 170 km × 170 km ≈ 29,000 km².
- World food is grown for today's population: global food supply is about 2,900–3,000 kcal per person per day for ~8 billion people (FAO), nowhere near enough for 14 times as many.
- Writing began about 5,200 years ago (cuneiform in Mesopotamia, about 3200 BCE). PRB counts roughly 9 billion births before 8000 BCE and about 46 billion between 8000 BCE and year 1; roughly 15–20 billion people (many) lived before writing existed, and most people after it could not read either.
- Thousands of languages spoken in the past are extinct (Sumerian, Akkadian, Etruscan and many unrecorded ones); about 7,000 are spoken today (Ethnologue).
- Before about 1800 most people were farmers or hunter-gatherers; PRB's numbers put roughly 97 of the 117 billion births before 1750, long before cars (1886) and the electric light bulb (1879).
- The narration is global: the effects are shown for every city in the world.
