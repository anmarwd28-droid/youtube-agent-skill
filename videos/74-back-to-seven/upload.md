## Facts (numbers and sources)
- A 7-year-old is about 120–125 cm tall, roughly 0.7 of an adult (WHO growth charts).
- Children learn the sound system of a new language much more easily; people who start before about age 10 usually reach a native-like accent (critical-period research, e.g. Flege et al.; grammar learning stays strong until about 17–18: Hartshorne, Tenenbaum & Pinker, Cognition 2018).
- Children aged 6–12 need 9–12 hours of sleep a night (American Academy of Sleep Medicine consensus, 2016).
- 7 × 8 = 56.
- One year is 1/7 of a 7-year-old's life and 1/35 of a 35-year-old's: the "proportional" explanation of why time seems to pass faster with age (Janet 1877; also linked to fewer new experiences as adults). Presented in the video as how time feels, not a measured law.
