What it shows, in order:

5. One step: the person drifts up and away for ever (dashed path), the camera pulls back; bars: escape speed ١٫٧ سم/ث vs a normal step ١٤٠ سم/ث.
6. Ropes on stakes along the ground; the person pulls along hand over hand, a crate strapped down. The side list shows here.
7. The planet's edge: the eye-to-horizon line «٦ أمتار»; then the person walks across the terminator from day into night.
8. A ball thrown slowly circles the planet like a tiny moon; a clock runs to ١:٣٠ and the ball hits the person's back.
9. The sphere deforms into a lumpy rock: «ليس كرة!», with a small diagram: a small rock vs a round body over ٦٠٠ km.

## Facts (as narrated)
Model: a rocky planet of radius R = 10 m (diameter 20 m) with Earth's mean density ρ = 5,514 kg/m³ (rock and iron). G = 6.674×10⁻¹¹.
- **Mass:** M = 4/3·π·R³·ρ = 4,189 m³ × 5,514 ≈ 2.31×10⁷ kg; GM ≈ 1.54×10⁻³ m³/s².
- **Circumference:** 2πR = 62.8 m ≈ «ثلاثة وستون مترًا».
- **Gravity 600,000 times weaker:** g = GM/R² ≈ 1.54×10⁻⁵ m/s². With the same density, g scales with R: g/g_Earth = R/R_Earth = 10 / 6,371,000 = 1.57×10⁻⁶, i.e. about 637,000 times weaker («ستمئة ألف مرة»).
- **Weight one tenth of a gram:** a 70 kg person weighs 70 × 1.57×10⁻⁶ kg-force ≈ 0.11 g-force.
- **The air escapes:** escape speed (below) is under 2 cm/s, while air molecules move at about 500 m/s at room temperature, so a body this small cannot hold any atmosphere; you would need a spacesuit all the time.
- **One step and you never come back:** escape speed v = √(2GM/R) = √(2 × 1.54×10⁻³ / 10) ≈ 0.0175 m/s ≈ 1.7 cm/s (under «سنتيمترين في الثانية»). A normal walking speed is about 1.4 m/s, 80 times more.
- **Ropes:** with no weight to hold you down, moving means pulling along fixed lines and tethering everything, as astronauts do on space walks.
- **Horizon 6 m:** for an eye 1.7 m above the ground, the line of sight to the horizon is d = √(2Rh + h²) = √(34 + 2.9) ≈ 6.1 m.
- **Day to night in a few moves:** a quarter of the circumference (sunlit centre to the edge of night) is only about 16 m; near the terminator a few metres take you into night.
- **The ball as a tiny moon, back after about an hour and a half:** circular orbit speed v = √(GM/r). Thrown about half a metre above the ground (r ≈ 10.5 m): v ≈ 1.2 cm/s («بنحو سنتيمتر في الثانية»), period T = 2πr/v ≈ 5,450 s ≈ 91 minutes. (Close to the surface the period depends only on density: T = √(3π/(Gρ)) ≈ 84 min, the same as an orbit skimming Earth's surface; the ISS takes about 92 min.)
- **It would not be round:** small bodies keep irregular shapes; rocky bodies only pull themselves into a sphere (hydrostatic equilibrium) at roughly 600 km or more across (Vesta, 525 km, is still not quite round). So a 20 m "planet" would be a lumpy rock, like the small asteroids, and would not count as a planet under the IAU definition.
- **Global framing:** the person stands for anyone in the world.
