## Facts (numbers and sources)
- Simple visual reaction time ≈ 0.2–0.25 s (Jain et al. 2015; classic studies); 10× faster ≈ 0.025 s.
- A cup falling for 0.025 s drops 0.5·g·t² ≈ 3 mm; adding hand movement it is still only a few centimetres.
- Driver perception-reaction time to brake ≈ 0.75 s for an expected event (Green, Transportation Human Factors 2000); at 100 km/h (27.8 m/s) that is ≈ 21 m; 10× faster ≈ 2 m.
- World Athletics rule 16.6: a start reaction under 0.100 s after the gun is a false start.
- Fast myelinated nerve fibres conduct up to ≈ 120 m/s (about 100 m/s in round numbers).
- Flies resolve about 250 flashes per second versus about 60 for humans (Healy et al., Animal Behaviour 2013) — about 4×, so a swatter looks slow to them.
