## Facts (approximate, as narrated)
- Earth orbits ~150 million km from the Sun, inside the habitable zone where liquid surface water is possible.
- Sunlight falls with the square of distance: 10% farther → 1/1.1² ≈ 83% (about a sixth less). Equilibrium temperature scales with distance^-½ → ~5% lower, roughly −14 °C on average before ice feedbacks.
- Kepler's third law: period ∝ distance^1.5 → 1.1^1.5 ≈ 1.15 → a year of ~420 days.
- Ice and snow reflect most sunlight (high albedo), which amplifies cooling (ice–albedo feedback).
- At Mars' distance (1.52 AU) Earth would receive ~43% of today's sunlight; the Sun would look about two-thirds as wide.
- Life can live around deep-sea hydrothermal vents without sunlight, even under an ice-covered ocean.
- The Moon recedes ~3.8 cm per year (lunar laser ranging) but stays bound to Earth.
- Earth's orbit has been stable for billions of years. Sizes and distances on screen are not to scale.
