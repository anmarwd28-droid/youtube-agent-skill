## Facts (numbers and sources)
- World population ≈ 8.2 billion (UN, 2025).
- Memory is not one system: episodic memories of your life depend on the hippocampus and nearby cortex, while skills (procedural memory: walking, riding a bicycle, playing an instrument) rely on the basal ganglia and cerebellum (Squire 2004; Milner, patient H.M.).
- People with severe amnesia usually keep language, reading and learned skills.
- Clive Wearing, a British musician and conductor, lost almost all his memory to herpes encephalitis in 1985 but could still play the piano and conduct (Wilson & Wearing 1995; Sacks, "The Abyss", 2007).
- Knowledge is also stored outside brains: in libraries (hundreds of millions of books) and on the internet.
