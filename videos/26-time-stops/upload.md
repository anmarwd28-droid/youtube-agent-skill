# ماذا لو توقّف الزمن ٥ ثوانٍ للجميع إلّا أنت؟ — Short #26

62 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps. Global framing.

What it shows, in order:
1. A busy city street (walkers, a boy's ball in the air, birds). On "للجميع" everything freezes and turns cold grey; only "you" (orange) stays in colour and keeps walking. A ripple of frozen time spreads out.
2. The 3D Earth: in an ordinary 5 seconds, a 5-second ring runs down, "+21" births, and "+220" lightning flashes over the night side.
3. A rainy street: rain, umbrellas, birds and the ball; then everything freezes. The raindrops hang as beads, and you walk on among frozen people.
4. A close-up of you: rays of light hang as dots in the air and stop short of your eye. Then total darkness.
5. Silence: sound rings frozen in the air. Only your heart beats, with an ECG trace (and the heartbeat sound).
6. Frozen air molecules (N₂ blue, O₂ red) form a wall. You push and can't move forward.
7. Lungs that can't fill: air ✕ at the mouth. Bars compare the 5 s you need with the usual 30–90 s breath-hold.
8. Time returns: colour, rain and movement come back. "لم يلاحظ أحد".
9. The dark frozen street and a gold countdown ring from 5 to 0.
10. Earth with the question mark and the subscribe button.

## Facts (as narrated)

- **The scenario:** a thought experiment. Everything outside your body stops for 5 seconds (light, air molecules, sound), and your own body keeps working.
- **About 20 births in 5 seconds:** about 130–135 million babies are born each year, about 4.2 per second (UN World Population Prospects), so about 21 in 5 s.
- **More than 200 lightning flashes in 5 seconds:** about 44 ± 5 flashes per second worldwide, including cloud and ground flashes (NASA OTD satellite data, Christian et al. 2003), so about 220 in 5 s.
- **Darkness:** you see only because photons enter your eye. If light stops in mid-air, no new light reaches the retina, so everything goes dark.
- **Silence:** sound is a pressure wave carried by moving air molecules. If they stop, no sound reaches your ears. Sound inside your own body (the heartbeat, carried through tissue and bone) can still be felt and heard.
- **Air like a wall:** normally air molecules move aside at hundreds of m/s. Frozen ones would not, so moving through them would be like pushing a solid. This is the standard answer to this thought experiment, not a measured fact.
- **Breathing:** with no air flowing in, you cannot breathe. A normal adult can hold their breath for about 30–90 seconds, so 5 seconds is safe.
- **Nobody notices:** for everyone else, no time has passed.
