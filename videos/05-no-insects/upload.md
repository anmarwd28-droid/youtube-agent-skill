## Facts (approximate, as narrated)
- About 75% of leading food crop types benefit from animal pollinators, mostly insects (FAO/IPBES).
- Apples, strawberries, cherries and cocoa depend heavily on insect pollination; wheat, rice and maize are wind-pollinated.
- Many birds, frogs and lizards feed mainly on insects.
- Insects are key decomposers of leaf litter, dung and carcasses, returning nutrients to soil.
