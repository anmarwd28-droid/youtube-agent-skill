## Facts (numbers and sources)
- Without operators, most power grids fail within hours to a couple of days (fuel, faults and load balancing need people) — "Life After People" (History, 2008), Alan Weisman, *The World Without Us* (2007).
- Canned food stays safe for years (USDA: high-acid 12–18 months best quality, low-acid 2–5 years; many cans remain edible far longer); bread moulds in days, opened/unrefrigerated milk spoils within days, refrigerated milk about a week.
- Wildlife moves into abandoned places quickly: Chernobyl exclusion zone (deer, foxes, wolves; Deryabina et al., Current Biology 2015) and animals in city streets during the 2020 lockdowns.
- Feral and hungry dogs are among the main threats in abandoned areas (Weisman).
- Cracks, weeds and trees take over roads within years (Pripyat; Weisman).
- Long-term social isolation raises mortality about as much as smoking up to 15 cigarettes a day (Holt-Lunstad et al., PLoS Medicine 2010; US Surgeon General advisory 2023).
- Solar panels keep producing for 25+ years; with batteries or direct daytime use, some lights can keep working for years.
