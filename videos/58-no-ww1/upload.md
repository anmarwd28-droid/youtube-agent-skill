## Facts (as narrated)
- **Sarajevo, summer 1914:** Archduke Franz Ferdinand was shot in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914; the war began on 28 July 1914. The street, car and people are generic drawings, not a reconstruction.
- **More than thirty countries:** about 32 states took part (Allies and Central Powers), with fighting or troops from every continent; the dots on the globe are capitals of countries at war (dominions and colonies of the empires also sent troops).
- **Four years, about 20 million dead:** the war lasted until 11 November 1918; estimates are 15–22 million military and civilian deaths (commonly "about 20 million"). Poison gas was first used on a large scale at Ypres in 1915.
- **The 1918 flu:** the influenza pandemic of 1918–1920 killed an estimated 50 million people (estimates 17–100 million), more than the war itself; troop movements, crowded camps and ships helped it spread.
- **Four empires fell:** the Russian (1917), German (1918), Austro-Hungarian (1918) and Ottoman (sultanate abolished 1922). The 1914 borders on the globe are simplified.
- **Russia:** the 1917 revolutions took place during the war; the Bolsheviks took power in November 1917 and the Soviet Union was founded in 1922. The palace and crowd are generic (plain red flags, no symbols).
- **The Middle East:** the secret Sykes–Picot agreement (1916) divided the Ottoman Arab provinces between Britain and France; after the war they became mandates (1920), the origin of the borders of Iraq, Syria, Lebanon, Jordan and Palestine. Zones and borders on the globe are simplified.
- **"Without this war, those empires might have stayed":** speculation, said as "ربّما".
- **Planes:** the warring countries built roughly 200,000+ aircraft during the war (France ≈ 68,000, Britain ≈ 55,000, Germany ≈ 48,000, Italy ≈ 20,000, the US ≈ 15,000, others more).
- **The first tank:** the British Mark I went into battle at Flers–Courcelette on the Somme on 15 September 1916.
- **The first blood bank:** in 1917 the US Army doctor Oswald H. Robertson stored blood (type O, with citrate and glucose) at a depot on the Western Front, the first blood bank.
- **Hitler:** Adolf Hitler served as a soldier (Gefreiter) in the Bavarian army; he was wounded in the leg in October 1916 at the Somme (and temporarily blinded by gas in October 1918). Before the war he lived as a poor painter in Vienna (1908–1913), selling small watercolours of the city.
- **Versailles and the second war:** the Treaty of Versailles was signed in the Hall of Mirrors on 28 June 1919. Hitler used the defeat and the treaty in his rise; World War II began in 1939, twenty years later, and killed 70–85 million people.
- **"Hitler might have stayed a poor painter":** speculation, said as "ربّما".
