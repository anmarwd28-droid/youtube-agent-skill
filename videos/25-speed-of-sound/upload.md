## Facts (as narrated)

- **Speed of sound:** about 343 m/s in air at 20 °C (about 1,235 km/h).
- **Fastest human:** Usain Bolt reached about 12.4 m/s (44.7 km/h) in his 2009 world-record 100 m. 1,235 / 44.7 ≈ 27.6, narrated as "about 28 times".
- **Sonic boom:** an object moving at or above Mach 1 creates a shock wave. Strong booms close by can break windows. Narrated with "قد" (may).
- **Heating:** air brought to rest against your face warms by ΔT = v² / (2cp) = 343² / (2 × 1005) ≈ 58 K. Narrated as "about 60 degrees".
- **Power:** aerodynamic drag power is P = ½ρ·CdA·v³.
  - With ρ = 1.2 kg/m³ and CdA ≈ 0.8 m² (an upright person): P ≈ 0.5 × 1.2 × 0.8 × 343³ ≈ 19 MW.
  - Near Mach 1 drag rises further, so this is a low estimate. Narrated as "about 20 megawatts, like a small power station".
- **Food:**
  - 19 MW for 60 s ≈ 1.16 GJ ≈ 278,000 kcal ≈ 139 days of a 2,000 kcal diet.
  - Muscles are only about 25% efficient, so the real figure would be several times higher.
  - Narrated as "more than four months".
- **Stopping:** braking at no more than 5 g (49 m/s²) from 343 m/s takes d = v² / 2a ≈ 1.2 km.
- **Orbit:**
  - Low-Earth orbital speed is about 7.9 km/s (the first cosmic velocity), ignoring air.
  - Satellites like the ISS orbit at about 7.7 km/s at about 400 km.
