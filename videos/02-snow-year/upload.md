## Facts used (approximate, as said in the narration)
- Wet, packed snow can weigh hundreds of kilograms per square metre on a roof.
- World grain stocks cover only a few months of consumption.
- Fresh snow reflects up to about 90% of sunlight (albedo feedback).
- During the last glacial maximum, sea level was about 120 m lower than today.
- Deep snow compresses into firn and then glacier ice.
- "Snowball Earth" episodes happened roughly 700 million years ago; volcanic CO2 ended them.
- Cold air holds much less water vapour, so a colder world gets less snow.
- Mount Baker (USA) recorded about 29 m of snow in one season (1998–99).
