## Facts (approximate, as narrated)
- About 8 billion people live on Earth today.
- Most power plants need people to supply fuel and balance the grid; without them most city lights would fail within hours (estimates range from hours to a couple of days).
- New York's subway pumps remove millions of litres of groundwater a day; without them parts of the system would flood within about 36 hours (Alan Weisman, *The World Without Us*).
- Without traffic and factories, urban air pollution drops within weeks (seen during the 2020 lockdowns, when NO₂ fell sharply).
- Animals reclaim abandoned places: around Chernobyl, evacuated in 1986, wolves, deer and introduced Przewalski's horses now thrive (Pripyat's Ferris wheel still stands).
- Within years plants break through cracked asphalt; within decades unmaintained wooden houses and steel bridges rust, rot and begin to fail; over centuries skyscrapers collapse and forests cover cities.
- Many plastics last hundreds of years or more.
- Our radio and TV broadcasts, sent since the 1920s, travel at the speed of light; the "bubble" is now about 100 light-years in radius (already past nearby stars such as Alpha Centauri, Sirius and Vega). Sizes and distances on screen are not to scale.
