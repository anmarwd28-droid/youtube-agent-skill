## Facts (approximate, as narrated)
- Banking, card payments and ATMs depend on network connections.
- Airline reservation and check-in systems depend on networks; outages cause delays.
- A global outage would cost billions of dollars per day.
- Undersea fibre-optic cables carry the vast majority (well over 90%) of intercontinental data.
