## Description
ماذا لو اختفت كل الحدود وأصبح العالم كله دولة واحدة؟ نحو ١٩٥ دولة تصبح بلداً واحداً: بلا جوازات سفر ولا تأشيرات، وبعملة واحدة للجميع.

## Facts (numbers and sources)
- About 195 countries: 193 member states of the United Nations plus 2 non-member observer states (UN, "Member States", un.org). The narration says «نحوُ مئةٍ وخمسٍ وتسعينَ دولة» (about 195).
- No passports or visas inside one country: travel inside a country needs no passport or visa; a real partial example is the Schengen Area, where 29 European countries have removed checks at their shared borders (European Commission, Schengen Area). The airport board shows cities on four continents.
- One currency: with a single currency there is nothing to exchange, so currency-conversion margins disappear (transfer fees can still exist). For scale, the World Bank's Remittance Prices Worldwide puts the global average cost of sending 200 dollars at about 6.4% (2024), part of which is the exchange-rate margin. On screen: «رسومُ التحويل … صفر» for the exchange office.
- One interest rate is hard: a single central bank sets one rate for all regions, which cannot suit a booming economy and a slow one at the same time ("optimum currency area" theory, Mundell 1961). The euro shows it: before 2008 low ECB rates helped fuel housing booms in some members (e.g. Spain, Ireland), and in the euro-area sovereign-debt crisis of 2010–2012 several members needed rescue programmes while they could not change their own interest or exchange rate (ECB; IMF; European Commission). Narration: «كما حدثَ لدولِ اليورو في أزمةِ الدَّيْن».
- World military spending: 2,718 billion US dollars in 2024, the highest ever recorded (SIPRI, "Trends in World Military Expenditure, 2024", April 2025). Narration: «نحوَ تريليونَينِ وسبعِمئةِ مليارِ دولارٍ سنويّاً»; on screen «≈ ٢٫٧ تريليون $ سنويّاً». «قد يذهبُ كثيرٌ منها للمدارسِ والمستشفيات» is a possibility (much of it "could" go), not a prediction.
- World population: about 8.2 billion (UN World Population Prospects 2024: 8.16 billion in mid-2024, about 8.2 billion in 2025). Narration rounds to «ثمانيةَ ملياراتِ إنسان»; on screen «٨٫٢ مليار».
- Languages: about 7,100–7,200 living languages are spoken today (Ethnologue, 2024–2025 editions: 7,164 / 7,159). Narration: «أكثرَ من سبعةِ آلافِ لغة».
- The biggest country by population today has about 1.45 billion people (UN WPP 2024: the most populous country had about 1.45 billion in 2024; the second about 1.42 billion). Narration: «نحوُ مليارٍ ونصف»; on screen «≈ ١٫٤٥ مليار». No country is named.
- Plugs and sockets: the International Electrotechnical Commission lists 15 plug and socket types in use around the world, types A to O (IEC, "World Plugs"). The wall shows six of them (A, B, C, D, G, I) turning into one type.
- Time cannot be unified: the Earth turns 15° an hour, so local solar time differs by 24 hours around the globe; when the Sun is overhead at noon on one side, it is around midnight on the opposite side (180° of longitude away). A single legal clock would not change day and night: some places would start work in the dark (basic astronomy; NIST/time-zone references).
- Global framing: the border post, airport, streets and square stand for any place in the world; no flags, no real people, no named governments or political sides.
