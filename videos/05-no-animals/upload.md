## Facts (approximate, as narrated)
- About three quarters of leading food crop types benefit from animal pollinators (FAO/IPBES).
- Corals are animals (cnidarians); without the coral animals reefs die.
- Earthworms aerate soil and improve its fertility.
- Many trees depend on birds and other animals to carry their seeds away from the parent tree.
- Meat, eggs, milk and honey all come from animals; plants, fungi and bacteria would remain.
