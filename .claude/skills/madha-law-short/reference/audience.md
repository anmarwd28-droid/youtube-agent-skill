## What it means
- **Ideas:** Sun/Moon/Earth-in-space ideas and "something disappears" ideas win, on views, retention and subscribers. Slow disaster stories such as the meteor lose people.
- **Traffic:** 95% of views come from the Shorts feed and 3.5% from search. The first 2 seconds decide everything, so open on the strongest image (Earth, Moon, Sun) with the question.
- **Countries:** the whole Arab world watches. In order: Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Algeria, Syria, Egypt, Morocco, Jordan, Tunisia, Lebanon, Libya. Keep simple Fusha and a global framing.
- **Audience:** adults, not kids. Ages 25–44 are 64% of viewers, and about 70% are male. Keep a serious science tone.
- **Times:** 10:00 and 18:00 Baghdad time fit the Gulf and Levant. The Maghreb is 2 hours behind, so 18:00 Baghdad is 16:00 there, which is fine.
